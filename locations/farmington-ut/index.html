Farmington FAQ

Questions about visiting

Practical answers about the clinic, hours and virtual options.

Where is the Everlume Health clinic in Farmington?

Our clinic is in Farmington, Utah 84025, a short drive from I-15 and Legacy Parkway. The full street address and parking details are listed on this page.

Do you have clinics in other Utah cities?

No. Farmington is our only physical clinic. Patients come to us from across Davis County and Northern Utah, and many visits can happen by secure video.

What are your clinic hours?

We are open Monday through Thursday from 8:00 am to 5:00 pm. Virtual consultations are scheduled within those hours.

Can I see a provider virtually instead of coming in?

Yes, for many concerns. Your free 15-minute consult is always virtual, and follow-ups such as hormone therapy check-ins or lab reviews can often be done by video. Exams and procedures like IUD placement happen in the clinic.

Is parking available at the Farmington clinic?

Yes. Free parking is available next to the building. We will confirm the exact details when the clinic address is finalized.
